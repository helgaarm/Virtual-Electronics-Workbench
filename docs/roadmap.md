# Roadmap

- **Phase A — complete in this milestone:** realistic 3D breadboard, camera inspection, real scale, snapping, selection, rotation, physical component rendering.
- **Phase B — complete in this milestone:** electrical extraction, DC source/resistor/switch/LED models, power control, live voltage/current feedback, SQLite project persistence.
- **Phase C — complete in this milestone:** deeper Test & Analysis workspace, persistent multimeter readings, and attachable probe workflow.
- **Phase D — complete in this milestone:** polarized capacitor, deterministic transient engine and clock, and analytically verified RC charge/discharge response.
- **Phase E — complete in this milestone:** two-channel oscilloscope and square/sine signal generator driven only by simulated samples.
- **Phase F hybrid-model milestone — complete:** reusable nonlinear diode/BJT simulation, internal subcircuits, DIP-8 packages, and an explicitly hybrid analogue NE555N with an astable starter. This is the scalable proof architecture, not completion of a die-like transistor-network NE555.
- **Original Phase 14 — complete:** frequency counter derives frequency and period from the shared waveform capture.
- **Original Phase 15 — complete:** initial eight-channel logic analyser with TTL thresholds, sample rate, Run/Stop/Single, trigger, and zoom on the shared simulation clock.
- **First follow-up:** NE555 monostable starter, then a faster optional behavioural model and a more detailed transistor-network model.
- **Later:** discrete catalogue parts such as 1N4148/BC547, op-amps, digital parts, other IC packages, firmware, experiments, faults, and hardware-in-the-loop.
